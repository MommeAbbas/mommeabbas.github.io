description: >
  A ROS 2 autonomy stack for a robot operating in a multi-robot delivery
  challenge. It combines autonomous exploration and SLAM-based mapping with
  tag-based dock discovery, mission execution, Nav2 navigation, docking,
  collision monitoring, and recovery. A YOLO11 robot detector and
  camera/lidar ranging feed particle filters that track nearby robots and
  predict possible trajectories and destination docks. The stack also
  includes optional prediction-aware risk and control components, plus a
  Gazebo-based evaluation harness for measuring deliveries, collisions, and
  prediction accuracy.
